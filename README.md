# JPN Industrial Trading Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

JPN Industrial Trading Pte Ltd supplies, rents, and services construction machinery and industrial equipment from its Singapore headquarters. The proposed homepage helps contractors, equipment buyers, and site teams find machinery, arrange rentals, or request technical support.

## Main Improvements

1. **Make Buy, Rent, and Service easy to find.** The current homepage leaves visitors to use the wider menu to find the right route. Three prominent entry points would give each customer a direct next step for purchasing equipment, arranging a rental, or requesting workshop support.
2. **Give equipment discovery a single starting point.** The proposed catalogue section would use product photography and clear categories to bring new and used machinery, MCWEL equipment, other brands, and spare parts into one browseable area. Dedicated enquiry links would help visitors ask about the equipment they need.
3. **Connect technical capabilities to the right contacts.** The proposed homepage would give repairs, reconditioning, and generator projects dedicated space. Enquiry links would use JPN’s existing contact channels for machinery sales, rentals, parts, and generator projects where relevant.

## Design Improvements

Prominent pathway buttons would help visitors choose their starting point. Consistent catalogue cards would pair product images with clear labels, while enquiry actions beside workshop and project details would make the next step easy to find.
