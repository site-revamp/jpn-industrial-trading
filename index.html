Company background

Established in Singapore. Growing with industry.

JPN's published company history traces its beginnings to 1988, with subsequent expansion into Malaysia and the development of its machinery and generator activities.

Read about JPN ↗
1988

JPN began trading in small construction machinery and equipment in Singapore.

1991–1997

Expanded activities into Malaysia through related industrial trading businesses.

2006

Relocated to its current Pandan Crescent premises.

2007

Introduced the MCWEL range of diesel generators, welding generators and water pumps.

2015

Expanded into generator projects and related servicing activities.
